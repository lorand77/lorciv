Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

LorCiv is a free strategy game site run by Lorand, an independent developer. This page explains what the site stores about you, who can see it, and how to have it removed. For questions or requests, write to lorciv@lorand77.dev.

What we store

  • Your account: your username, your password, and the date you joined. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can read them. We don't ask for your email address, your real name or anything else about you.
  • Your login session: a record on the server that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days, or until you log out.

Who can see it

  • Other signed-in players can see your username.
  • Nothing is visible to people who aren't signed in.

We don't sell your data, show ads or share your data with anyone, unless the law requires us to.

Cookies and tracking

LorCiv sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is needed for the site to work. The site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

IP addresses and logs

The server keeps logs so that we can find and fix problems. They record what your account does on the site (for example signing in or out) with the time, whether it worked, and your username and account number. They never contain your password.

We use your IP address to limit repeated login and sign-up attempts, and for that it is held in memory for about 15 minutes. It is also written to the logs when someone signs in, tries to sign in or signs up, so that we can deal with password guessing and abuse. It is not recorded for anything else you do on the site.

Logs are deleted after 14 days and are not included in backups.

Where it is kept

The site runs on a server at Amazon Web Services. The database is backed up every night to Backblaze B2, and each backup is kept for up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • To delete your account, or to get a copy of what we hold about you, email lorciv@lorand77.dev with your username. Because we don't know your email address, we may ask you to prove that the account is yours.
  • When an account is deleted, its sessions are removed and its name is freed. Copies in backups disappear as those backups expire, within 90 days, and log entries that mention the account within 14 days.

Children

LorCiv is not for children under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also announce it on the site.
